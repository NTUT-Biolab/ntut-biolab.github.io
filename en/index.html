Tun-Wen Pai白敦文

Vice President, NTUT · Professor, Dept. of CSIE

Prof. Tun-Wen Pai received his Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Duke University. He has served as Department Chair and as Director of the Center for Genomics and Bioinformatics at National Taiwan Ocean University, and as Department Chair of CSIE and Director of International Affairs at NTUT. His research focuses on bioinformatics, medical informatics, early childhood developmental delay diagnosis and cancer biomarker discovery.
